Give your AI agent a voice.

Voice for AI Agents lets almost any AI agent speak out loud, in a natural voice. In development.

What it does.

In development. Not released yet.

AI agents are good at doing work and bad at telling you about it. They finish a long task and wait silently for you to look at the screen. Voice for AI Agents lets them say it out loud.

It is an MCP server. MCP (the Model Context Protocol) is the common way AI agents connect to tools, so any agent that supports it, such as Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Cursor, can connect once and then speak: spoken replies, alerts when a task finishes, and reading text aloud.

The speech engine is swappable. The agent makes the same request whichever engine is behind it, so the voice can improve without changing anything on the agent side.

Speech engines.

Pick the trade-off between quality, cost and privacy.

  • Built in

    Windows voices

    The voices that come with Windows. Nothing to install, works offline. Robotic, so it is the fallback.

    Offline · Free

  • Local

    Kokoro

    A small neural model that runs on the PC. Fast, with dozens of built-in voices.

    Offline · Free

  • Local

    Chatterbox

    A larger neural model that runs on the PC's graphics card and can copy a voice from a short recording. The text and the recording never leave the PC.

    Offline · Free · Voice copying

  • Hosted

    ElevenLabs

    A hosted speech service with very natural voices, including voices cloned in your own account. The clips on this page were made with it.

    Hosted · Paid per use

  • Open

    Any compatible speech server

    Any server that offers an OpenAI-compatible speech endpoint can be plugged in. Not yet tested against a real server.

    Local or hosted

Where it stands.

An honest status.

  • Early build. It runs on the founder's own PC and is tested in-house. It has not been released and has no outside users.
  • Works today: speaking through the PC's speakers, choosing the output device, saving each clip as an audio file, and copying a voice from a recording.
  • Not built yet: listening (speech to text), streaming audio, and stopping speech mid-sentence.
  • The goal: speech that sounds indistinguishable from a person, available to almost any agent.

A voice should only be copied with the consent of the person it belongs to. The voice on this page is the founder's own.
