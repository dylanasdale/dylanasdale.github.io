Asdale Dynamics, LLC

An RN-founded AI product company based in Avon, Indiana.

What we do.

Products page →

Asdale Dynamics mainly builds its own AI products. Two are in development:

  • An AI model router for the desktop: a desktop app that routes each task to the best free or low-cost AI model available, to cut AI API costs.
  • Patient-advocacy briefs: an app that turns a patient's treatment history into evidence-linked briefs for clinic visits.

Client work is secondary: AI agents, multi-model pipelines, workflow automations and custom web apps. The company's long-term research directions are brain-computer interfaces for treatment-resistant depression, and AI-guided pharmaceutical development, with a focus on psychopharmacology, to be carried out in collaboration with physicians.
