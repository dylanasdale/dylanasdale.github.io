Asdale Dynamics, LLC

An Indiana company making AI practical — books, free guides, and technology services.

What we do.

Asdale Dynamics, LLC provides AI and technology services and publishes practical, plain-English AI guides. Our work includes:

  • AI books and guides — including The Massive AI Manual and the free, print-ready Guides for Everything AI library.
  • AI consulting and services — helping real people and small teams get real results from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and cloud tools like AWS.
  • New projects and experiments — tools and guides in progress, announced first on the projects page.

The company.

Asdale Dynamics, LLC is a domestic Indiana limited liability company, formed on October 21, 2026 and based in Avon, Indiana. It is founded and run by Dylan Asdale, author of The Massive AI Manual.
