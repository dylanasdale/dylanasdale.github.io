A nurse who builds AI products.

Dylan Asdale, RN, BSN is the Founder and Sole Member of Asdale Dynamics, LLC.

The founder.

Registered nurse turned AI builder.

Dylan Asdale is a registered nurse (RN, BSN) who taught himself to build with AI tools. He founded Asdale Dynamics, LLC in October 2026 to turn that work into products, and he runs the company as its Founder and Sole Member from Avon, Indiana.

His nursing background shapes what the company builds. One of its two products in development is a patient-advocacy app that turns a patient's treatment history into evidence-linked briefs for clinic visits. The other is a desktop app that routes each task to the best free or low-cost AI model available, to cut AI API costs. More on both is on the products page.

The company's long-term research direction is AI-guided pharmaceutical development, with a focus on psychopharmacology, to be carried out in collaboration with physicians.
